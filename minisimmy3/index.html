Prestige System

Prestige resets the entire simulation (creatures, food, time) but has powerful benefits:

  • Your Gene Vault is fully preserved.
  • A portion of your High Roller Points is converted into Aetherium Crystals.
  • You get a starting bonus of High Roller Points after reset.

This is the main long-term progression loop. The more powerful the creatures you have in your Gene Vault, the stronger your next run becomes.

The Furnace

The Furnace lets you burn Aetherium Crystals for a massive temporary boost:

  • Burning crystals gives a x100 score multiplier.
  • Each crystal burns for 5 minutes.
  • You can add more crystals to extend/reset the timer and increase the bonus.

This is one of the fastest ways to earn large amounts of High Roller Points, which then convert into more Aetherium on your next Prestige.

Saving Your Progress

MiniSimmy3 automatically saves your progress using your browser’s local storage:

  • Eggs in your Inventory
  • Gene Vault contents
  • High Roller Points and Aetherium Crystals

You can also manually export your eggs as a JSON file from the Inventory and import them later. This system is designed to carry your best creatures into future sessions or the upcoming Ranch game.
