MiniSimmy3 Game Guide

Complete reference for mechanics, systems, and progression

Overview

MiniSimmy3 is a modular creature evolution simulation. Creatures move through a vortex, gather energy from food zones, reproduce, and evolve through modules. Your goal is to develop strong bloodlines, preserve them in the Gene Vault, and progress through Prestige and the Furnace economy.

The Simulation

Vortex

A powerful spiraling force pulls creatures toward the center. Mover and Explorer modules help creatures resist or escape it.

Food Zones

Green zone (day) and Blue zone (night). Creatures are attracted to them. Harvester modules increase attraction strength.

Energy & Reproduction

Creatures lose energy over time. When they have high energy and low cooldown, they reproduce. Resistant modules reduce energy drain.

Pheromones

Communicator modules cause creatures to release pheromones that attract others, creating social grouping behavior.

Modules & Specializations

Harvester
Stronger attraction to food zones. Excellent for energy gain.
Sensor
Detects and avoids predators. Provides awareness and survival advantage.
Mover
Better vortex escape. Helps creatures resist being pulled to the center.
Resistant
Reduces energy drain. Very strong for long-term survival.
Explorer
Encourages spreading out and exploration. Good for genetic diversity.
Communicator
Releases pheromones that attract other creatures. Creates social behavior.

Predators

Predators emerge under social pressure or through conversion. They hunt low-module creatures and can spread their status. Having Sensor modules helps creatures avoid them.

Gene Vault

The Gene Vault lets you preserve powerful creatures across Prestige resets. Extract strong creatures into eggs, store them in your Inventory, then move them into the Gene Vault. Only the first 2 slots are unlocked initially.

Economy & Furnace

High Roller Points (HRP): Earned from simulation performance (population, predators, time).

Aetherium Crystals: Gained when you Prestige. Converted from HRP.

Furnace: Burn Aetherium Crystals to activate a powerful x100 score multiplier for 5 minutes.

Prestige

Prestige resets the simulation but preserves your Gene Vault. You convert some of your HRP into Aetherium Crystals. This is the main long-term progression system.

Saving Your Progress

Eggs are automatically saved to your browser. You can also manually Export your eggs as a JSON file and Import them later. This system is designed to carry your best creatures into future sessions or the upcoming Ranch game.
