Creature Inspector

Clicking on any creature opens the Inspector panel, where you can see:

  • Name and Generation
  • Current Energy
  • Number and types of Modules
  • Visual representation of the creature and its modules

From the Inspector you can also extract the creature into an egg for storage in your Inventory or Gene Vault.

Overall Goals

The main goals in MiniSimmy3 are:

  1. Develop strong, specialized creatures through modules.
  2. Extract the best ones into eggs and store them in the Gene Vault.
  3. Use the Furnace to multiply your score and earn more Aetherium.
  4. Prestige regularly to convert progress into long-term power.
  5. Build up powerful bloodlines that survive across many Prestiges.

The ultimate goal is to create legendary creature lineages that you can carry forward into future content (such as the planned Ranch game).
