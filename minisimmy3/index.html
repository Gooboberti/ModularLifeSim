Controls & Interface

Click on a creature to inspect it in detail.

Speed controls at the top right let you run the simulation faster or pause it.

Prestige button resets the world but keeps your Gene Vault and converts progress into Aetherium.

Inventory stores extracted eggs. You can move them to the Gene Vault or delete them.

Gene Vault preserves your best creatures across Prestige resets.

Furnace lets you burn Aetherium for a temporary x100 score multiplier.

Tips for Success

• Focus on developing creatures with multiple modules — they survive longer and reproduce better.

• Use the Gene Vault early. Strong bloodlines are your long-term power.

• The Furnace is extremely powerful. Save Aetherium for big score pushes.

• Sensor modules are excellent for survival once predators appear.

• Prestige regularly once you have good creatures in the Vault.
