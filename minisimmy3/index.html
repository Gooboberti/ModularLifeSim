How Modules Work

Every creature can have multiple modules. Each module gives a specialization bonus:

  • Harvester — Stronger pull toward food zones (green during day, blue at night).
  • Sensor — Detects nearby predators and helps the creature avoid them.
  • Mover — Better at escaping the central vortex.
  • Resistant — Loses energy much more slowly.
  • Explorer — Tends to wander more and explore the map.
  • Communicator — Releases pheromones that attract other creatures.

Modules can combine. Having multiple of the same type makes the effect stronger. Higher tier modules (from merging) are significantly more powerful.
