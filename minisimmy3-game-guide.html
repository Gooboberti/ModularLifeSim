Overview

MiniSimmy3 is a modular creature evolution simulation. Creatures move through a vortex, gather energy, reproduce, and evolve through modules. Your long-term goal is to develop strong bloodlines, preserve them in the Gene Vault, and progress through Prestige and the Furnace economy.

Core Simulation Loop

Every frame the simulation does the following:

  • Updates day/night cycle (affects food zones)
  • Updates pheromones, vortex particles, and all creatures
  • Handles reproduction and death
  • Draws everything on screen
  • Updates UI (population, score, time, etc.)
Creatures & Modules

Every creature has a set of modules that define its behavior and strengths. Modules can be stacked and upgraded through merging.

Module Types
Harvester
Stronger attraction to food zones. Excellent for energy gain and population growth.
Sensor
Detects and avoids predators. Provides strong survival advantage once predators appear.
Mover
Better at escaping the central vortex. Helps creatures resist being pulled inward.
Resistant
Reduces energy drain over time. Very strong for long-term survival and reproduction.
Explorer
Encourages spreading out across the map. Good for genetic diversity.
Communicator
Releases pheromones that attract other creatures. Creates social grouping behavior.

Higher tier modules (created by merging) give significantly stronger effects.

Vortex & Environment

A powerful spiraling force constantly pulls creatures toward the center. Creatures with Mover or Explorer modules can resist or escape it more effectively.

Food zones (green during day, blue at night) provide energy. Harvester modules increase attraction to these zones.

Gene Vault

The Gene Vault is the core long-term progression system. You can extract strong creatures into eggs and store them permanently across Prestige resets.

Only the first 2 slots are unlocked by default. Additional slots can be purchased with Aetherium Crystals in future updates.

Creatures stored in the Gene Vault can influence future generations when Prestige babies are spawned.

Economy & Furnace

High Roller Points (HRP): Main score earned from simulation performance.

Aetherium Crystals: Earned when you Prestige. Used in the Furnace and for unlocking Gene Vault slots.

Furnace: Burn Aetherium Crystals to activate a powerful x100 score multiplier for 5 minutes. You can add more crystals to extend the timer.

Prestige System

Prestige resets the simulation but preserves your Gene Vault. You convert some of your HRP into Aetherium Crystals and receive a starting bonus for the next run.

This is the main long-term progression loop. The stronger the creatures in your Gene Vault, the stronger your next run becomes.
