Saving System

MiniSimmy3 uses browser localStorage to persist your progress between sessions.

What gets saved:
- All eggs in your Inventory
- Gene Vault contents
- High Roller Points
- Aetherium Crystals

The system automatically saves after important actions (extracting eggs, moving to vault, prestige, etc.). You can also manually save from the Game Guide.

In the future, the saving system is designed to support exporting your full progress (including Gene Vault data) for use in the planned Ranch game.

User Interface Overview

Top Bar: Shows current population, simulation time, High Roller Points, Aetherium Crystals, and egg count.

Creature Inspector: Click any creature to see detailed information including generation, modules, energy, and behavior.

Inventory: Stores extracted eggs. You can move eggs to the Gene Vault or delete them.

Gene Vault: Long-term storage for powerful creatures across Prestige resets.

Furnace: Burn Aetherium to temporarily multiply your score gain.
