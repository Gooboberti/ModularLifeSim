1. Overview

MiniSimmy3 is a modular creature evolution simulation focused on long-term progression, gene preservation, and emergent behavior.

Creatures exist in a contained environment with a central vortex. They move, consume energy, reproduce, and evolve through modules that grant special abilities. Over time, you can extract strong creatures into eggs, store them in the Gene Vault, and carry powerful lineages across Prestige resets.

The core loop is: Observe → Extract strong creatures → Preserve in Gene Vault → Prestige → Repeat with stronger starting conditions.

2. Core Simulation Loop

The simulation runs continuously. Every frame the game performs these steps:

1. Environment Update
Day/Night cycle toggles every ~550 frames. This switches which food zone is active (green = day, blue = night).
2. Pheromone Update
Pheromones decay over time. Creatures with Communicator modules release new ones.
3. Vortex & Particle Update
Vortex particles are updated. They create the visual spiraling effect and influence creature movement.
4. Creature Update
Every creature updates its position, energy, behavior, and checks for reproduction or death.
5. Rendering
All creatures, particles, pheromones, and food zones are drawn.
6. UI Update
Top bar stats (population, time, score, crystals, egg count) are refreshed.
3. Creatures & Modules (Deep Dive)

Every creature is defined by its modules. Modules determine specialization, behavior, and survival strategy.

Module Effects in Detail
Harvester
Increases attraction strength to active food zones. Stacking multiple Harvesters creates very strong food-seeking behavior. Higher tiers make creatures extremely efficient at gathering energy.
Sensor
Gives creatures the ability to detect nearby predators and actively move away from them. This is one of the best defensive modules once predators start appearing.
Mover
Improves the creature’s ability to resist and escape the central vortex. Very useful for maintaining population spread.
Resistant
Reduces the rate at which energy is lost. This is one of the strongest modules for long-term survival and allowing creatures to reproduce more times before dying.
Explorer
Adds random movement variation. Creatures with Explorer modules tend to spread out more across the map instead of clustering.
Communicator
Causes creatures to periodically release pheromones. Other creatures are attracted to these signals, creating social grouping behavior.
How Modules Combine

Creatures can have multiple different modules. The more modules of the same type they have, the stronger that specialization becomes. When three modules of the same type exist on one creature, they can merge into a higher tier module.

Example: A creature with 3 Harvester modules can merge them into 1 Tier 2 Harvester. This is much stronger than three separate Tier 1 modules.
4. Vortex Mechanics

The central vortex is one of the defining environmental forces. It constantly pulls creatures toward the center of the map.

Creatures with high Mover or Explorer module counts can resist this pull more effectively. The vortex also creates interesting gameplay tension — clustering near the center is dangerous but sometimes necessary for reproduction or energy.

5. Gene Vault (Core Progression System)

The Gene Vault is the most important long-term system in MiniSimmy3.

How it works:
You can extract strong creatures from the simulation into eggs. These eggs can then be placed into the Gene Vault. Creatures in the Gene Vault survive Prestige resets.

In future updates, creatures stored in the Gene Vault will be able to influence the genetics of Prestige babies (prestige spawns), allowing you to carry forward desirable traits across runs.

Currently only 2 slots are unlocked. More slots will be unlockable with Aetherium Crystals.

6. Economy & Furnace

High Roller Points (HRP) is the main score. It is earned based on population, time survived, predator kills, and other factors.

Aetherium Crystals are earned when you Prestige. They are the premium currency used for the Furnace and future Gene Vault upgrades.

Furnace System:
You can burn Aetherium Crystals in the Furnace to activate a temporary x100 score multiplier. Each crystal burned lasts for 5 minutes. Adding more crystals resets the timer and increases the total multiplier.
